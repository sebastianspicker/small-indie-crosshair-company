This note audits the research learners against the 0.10.0 state of knowledge,
adds an export-state learner, and turns the places where learners, other
converters and our solver disagree into a ranked list of proposed fixes to the
public algorithm. Nothing here changes `lib/`; learned code still reaches the
app only through a reviewed, versioned model change.

### Export-state learner (design)

- **Labels.** `infer()` with `community-static-2026-10` (`community-static-v4`),
  read back through `nativeCommands`: length, thickness, gap, screen height,
  drawoutline and outline alpha.
- **Groups.** 400 development and 80 challenge setting groups **[synthetic]**.
  The core strata are the September 29 accuracy strata. The seven appearance
  strata are: outline off (with and without the share-code zero flag), sub-1
  width, share-code rounded zero, CFG zero, share-code steps 0.5–3, and
  continuous widths. Alpha is drawn independently.
- **Rows per group.** Five equal heights (720/960/1080/1440/2160), plus three
  cross-height scopes × two goals, × four dot/T flags = 44 rows.
- **Independence.** All 996 earlier learner groups (400 accuracy, 400
  structured, 196 community) and every corpus and preset triple are excluded
  before drawing. Partitions use
  `hash(export-v1:triple)%10` and are fixed before fitting.
- **Holdouts.**
  - The synthetic test set (86 groups).
  - A challenge set (80 groups) at unseen heights (768/1024 and two new
    cross scopes) with boundary outline widths (1e-7, 0.4999999, 0.9999999,
    1.0000001, 2.9999999).
  - The corpus (134 exportable static records × 7 equal heights) and the same
    records at the three cross-height scopes **[corpus]**.
  - Presets (8 × 7).
- **Model.**
  - Core: the structured ranker refit on the new groups (capacity 24×3 or
    48×4, chosen on validation).
  - Appearance: a boosted-tree head predicting the drawoutline mode and whether
    the outline alpha is the old crosshair alpha. The candidate set is
    {255, crosshair alpha}, and training stops on any label outside it.
  - Neither part reads a label, solver winner or overlap score.
- **Design amendment, disclosed.**
  - Run 1 (seed 2026100201) never combined *outline off* with the share-code
    rounded-zero flag. The corpus has 44 such records, so run 1 scored 68.66%
    export fidelity on the corpus (294 drawoutline errors; synthetic test
    97.78%).
  - Run 2 (seed 2026100203) samples that flag in the outline-off stratum. No
    capacity, feature or split rule changed.
  - The corpus is therefore a **re-evaluation, not a pristine holdout**. The
    run 2 synthetic test and challenge groups were drawn after the amendment.

## 8. Ranked public-algorithm findings

Proposed for delegation; none was applied in this note. Line numbers are the
working tree on 2026-10-02 (0.10.0). F1, F3, F4 and F5 were applied in 0.11.0
(section 9); F2 and F6 still wait for captures.

### F1 — Tie-break scores a whole-shape shift that no cvar can remove (proposed fix)

- **Evidence [synthetic], [corpus].**
  - `conversion-accuracy.json` `alignedTieAudit`: of 8,640 grid cases, 2,108
    are tied and **468** pick a different tuple under translation-aligned IoU
    (mean aligned gain 0.357; 236 of 384 tied cases at 720 → 1080, authored
    960, screen goal).
  - Export test: 233/796 tied rows. Real settings at cross heights: 74/222
    tied rows, 48 of 134 records.
  - Example **[corpus]**: ZywOo 1.5/0/-3 at 720 → 1080 (authored 960, screen
    goal) exports 3/**2**/2 with aligned IoU 0.385. The tied 3/**1**/3 matches
    at 1.000.
  - The learner-disagreement audit independently flags the same cluster
    (section 4).
- **Cause.**
  - `lib/solver/community-search.js:43` scores each tied candidate with
    `visual.score(predicted).iou` (`lib/solver/visual.js:9`), which compares
    absolute pixels.
  - The old target is centred at `-floor(W/2)` and new shapes at `-ceil(W/2)`,
    so a perfect odd-width shape scores below 1. The tie-break then prefers
    shapes that overlap the misplaced centre, typically a thicker bar or dot.
- **Affected inputs.** Cross-height conversions with tied lengths, widths or
  gaps. Equal-height conversions have no ties and do not change, and neither
  do the 24 crosshair.club tuples (all equal-height).
- **Change.** In `searchCommunity` (`community-search.js:43` and the
  comparison at `:50`), rank tied candidates by IoU maximised over whole-shape
  integer shifts within ±1 px. An equivalent option is to normalise both shapes
  to one centring before comparing. Keep `convertedFit` raw for reporting.
  This changes outputs, so it needs a new model version
  (`community-static-v5`) and a minor version bump.
- **Regression tests:**
  - ZywOo 1.5/0/-3, 720→1080/960 screen → 3/1/3.
  - Size 0, thickness 0, gap -5.5, dot, same scope → thickness 1.
  - Equal-height corpus outputs unchanged.
  - Odd and even widths, zero size and thickness, negative gaps.

### F2 — Gap origin for odd widths is unverified and decides 41% of corpus rows (capture first)

- **Evidence [stated], [corpus].** The tools split three ways (section 5), and
  section 6 sizes the effect.
- **Affected inputs.** Every odd exported thickness: 388/945 corpus rows,
  46/135 records at 1080.
- **Change.** None until a capture decides it. If captures show
  `floor(W/2)`, change the odd-width far-edge term in
  `lib/geometry/community.js` `communityForward` (`far = near - width % 2`)
  together with the solver radius.
- **Test.** A capture-backed holdout in the
  [capture protocol](capture-protocol-2026-10-02.md), with odd thickness at gap
  0 and gap 4.

### F3 — Share-code colour indices 6–7 make conversion fail (proposed fix)

- **Evidence [corpus].** pro-064 (sFade8, `CSGO-PXGLy-zn3hS-SbLyx-hOUJZ-X3nQD`)
  decodes to colour 7 with RGB 255/255/255. `parseLegacyText` accepts it;
  `infer` throws "Unsupported legacy color preset."
- **Cause.** `lib/settings/sharecode.js:45` decodes `bytes[10] & 7` (0–7),
  but `lib/settings/native.js:31-32` (`rgba`) only knows presets 0–4 plus
  custom 5.
- **Change.** Decide how the old game read 6–7. Either map 6–7 to the stored
  RGB (as custom 5, consistent with the record's RGB) with a warning, or
  reject them at import with a clear message instead of failing at conversion.
- **Test.** That record's code converts, or is rejected at import with a
  message; colours 0–5 are unchanged.

### F4 — Outline wider than one pixel cannot be reproduced; say so (proposed warning)

- **Evidence [synthetic], [source-derived].** Every width above 1 differs
  (section 7). Old 1.5 draws 2 px top-left and 1 px bottom-right; old 2 draws
  2/2; old 3 draws 3/3. The assumed mode 1 draws 1/1. No corpus record is
  affected.
- **Change.** In `lib/solver/community.js:76`, when
  `legacyOutlineExtent(s)` differs from `nativeOutlineExtent(s)` and the mode is
  1, emit a dedicated warning (for example `outline-width-reduced`, giving the
  old pixel widths) next to `outline-replacement-unverified`. Do the same in
  the manual model.
- **Test.** Outline widths 1.25, 1.5, 2 and 3 warn; 0.5, 1 and share-code 0
  do not.

training time (the accuracy training now takes about 3.5 minutes).

**Scale-free regime variant (ML-2).** The diagnosis came from the feature
definitions, not from challenge scores: the `regime` variant appends all 40
cross-check input features, many of them sizes in old or current pixels
(`oldLength`, `targetNear`, `idealLength`, the alpha) that grow with the
screen heights, and its axis features measure errors against the analytic
baseline although it predicts residuals from the regime baseline. The new
variant `regimeAxis` keeps the quantized layout, computes the per-axis
fractions, pixel errors and loss deltas on the regime target against the
regime baseline, and adds only the 18 scale-free regime features (kinds,
flags, parities, fractions, candidate counts, plus-against-T overlaps).
Validation picks it over `regime` (98.68% against 98.24%). Afterwards, on
splits already seen (regression, not evidence):

| Variant | Test | Interior | Exterior | Rendered exact, exterior |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| quantized | 96.27% | 78.91% | 76.80% | 87.58% |
| regime | 97.75% | 77.58% | 71.88% | 90.23% |
| scale-free regime | 97.50% | 80.70% | 74.30% | 91.17% |

It narrows the regime variant's loss on the shifted challenges (interior
+3.12, exterior +2.42 points) at a 0.25-point cost on the test split, and
beats the quantized variant on interior and on rendered exterior masks, but
not on exact exterior tuples. The weakness is reduced, not removed. The
cross-check regression columns still score the `regime` and `quantized`
models, which are unchanged in kind.

**Provenance freeze.** `crosscheck-emulator.json` now records
`training.freeze`: SHA-256 hashes of `lib/solver/ml-crosscheck.js` and
of the research trainer's feature code, and of the ranker and head capacity
lists. The trainer (and its byte-identical check) refuses to run when they
differ; an explicit refreeze records a deliberate change, which belongs in
this note. The hashes were first recorded on 2026-10-03, after the fresh sets had
been scored, so they guard later changes only.
